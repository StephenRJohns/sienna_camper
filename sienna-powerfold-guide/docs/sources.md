## Caveats carried into the document

- Kit contents and switch behaviour vary between sellers of superficially identical kits.
- The 13 mm / 19 mm sizes come from an installer's written account cross-referenced
  against photos, not from a spec sheet. Verify against the tool in hand.
- The extra-features wire behaviour (auto windows on lock, one-touch windows, hazard
  flash on park) is reported by owners, not documented by the seller.
